About Me

I am a curious and fun-loving person who enjoys exploring new things and making good memories. Being a Gen-Z, I naturally love watching anime, web series, and movies in my free time. Anime has actually helped me improve my English a lot. I have learned many new words, phrases, and expressions just by watching the shows I enjoy.

I also love travelling and exploring new places, especially hill stations. I enjoy the peaceful atmosphere, beautiful views, and the feeling of being away from the usual routine. Places like Trikund and other hilly destinations really interest me because I love exploring nature and experiencing something different. I also want to learn new skills, especially playing the guitar, as it seems both fun and creative.

Along with enjoying life, I want to focus on improving myself. One of my main goals is to work on my physique and become fitter and stronger. I want to build a healthy lifestyle while still enjoying the things I love. I believe life should be a balance of having fun, exploring new places, learning new skills, and continuously becoming a better version of myself.
